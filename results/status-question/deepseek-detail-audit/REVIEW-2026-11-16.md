## Corrected counts

DeepSeek gave a retraction year for 18 of its 28 answers on retracted papers. All 18 years are wrong: each falls before 2026, and every registry retraction date is between 2 July and 7 November 2026. The other 10 answers give no notice year. DeepSeek publishes no training cutoff (released 10 Sep 2026, updated 17 Oct), so exposure to some retractions cannot be ruled out. Even so, none of its notice years is right. The audit illustrates invented detail; it does not measure an error rate comparable with other models.

## Not checked

Original-article details (journal, year, volume) were not checked systematically. Discrepancies were seen in passing, for example the claimed journal for pair 73381. Notice DOIs other than the two above were not resolved. The other 24 control answers, which all claim a retraction, were not checked for details.
